The Collection

Every piece has lived a life before yours.

Lightings

There is something about old light - the way it pools amber across a room, unhurried. Our lighting pieces carry the warmth of workshops long gone: wrought iron floor lamps, hand-blown glass pendants, and brass sconces that once dressed the walls of estates. Each one throws shadows worth keeping.

Wrought iron lamps, hand-blown pendants, and brass sconces carrying the warmth of workshops long gone.

Accessories

The smallest objects hold the longest stories. A ceramic dish that circled a morning table for forty years. A bronze letter opener still sharp with purpose. Our accessories are the quiet details that shift a space from decorated to inhabited — collected slowly, placed with intention.

Ceramic dishes, bronze openers, and quiet details that turn a space from decorated to inhabited.

Wall Decor

A bare wall is a silence waiting to be filled. We source mirrors that have reflected decades of faces, framed prints pulled from print rooms and private libraries, and hand-carved panels that carry the grain of their original forest. Your walls deserve something that has already earned its place.

Mirrors, framed prints, and hand-carved panels for walls that deserve something already earned.

Furniture

These are not replicas. A writing desk with a drawer that sticks just slightly. A daybed in faded velvet that sags in the one right place. Our furniture pieces are built from eras when durability was a form of respect — for the maker, the material, and everyone who would sit in it after.

Writing desks, daybeds, and furniture built from eras when durability meant respect.

Who We Are

We don't sell objects.
We return them.

Relique began as a feeling — the kind you get standing in an old room, surrounded by things that have outlived their first owners. Every piece we carry has been touched by time, restored without being erased, and chosen for the life it still has left to give.
